Press run, or punch + kick together, to start running. Punch or kick while running to attack; push the opposite direction to brake. Tap punch or kick while down to help recover.

Walk close to grab. When you control the hold, punch to enter a headlock or kick to whip. From a headlock, press punch, kick or both to choose a move; use directions to carry. Press punch or kick while held to attempt a headlock reversal.

Controller support ready. Connect a pad and press a button.

Pad · Left stick / D-pad move · Bottom button A / × punch · Right B / ○ kick · Left X / □ run · Top Y / △ pin · Menu / Options start / pause. In selection, left/right choose your wrestler and up/down choose the opponent; hold a shoulder button to choose their partners. View / Share returns to selection while paused.

Tag: walk toward your partner’s corner and wait for them to approach. Press punch or kick beside the waiting partner to tag. Control follows the incoming wrestler.

This early tag-team playtest uses recovered input, AI, movement, contact and tag routines with the original artwork and sound commands. Kick near a prone opponent to cover them; tap punch or kick to escape. Some moves and match continuations still need work and offer a restart. Complete arcade rules, outside-ring behavior, Royal Rumble, later matches, effects and audio routing remain under reconstruction.

Paired poses use both selected wrestlers. Some poses exist only for specific matchups. Unused table entries have no artwork.

Enable audio to hear the recovered effects and FM music.

The original music and effect sequences run in JavaScript. The FM renderer uses a JavaScript build of the BSD-licensed ymfm library. Gameplay sound routing is still being reconstructed.
